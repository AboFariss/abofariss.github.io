Claude Health MCP

A personal, single-user tool that reads its owner's own Google Health (Fitbit) data, read-only, for personal records.

Privacy policy

This app is used only by its owner. Data is read with read-only permissions, stored only on the owner's own computer, and never sold, shared or sent to third parties. Access can be revoked at any time at myaccount.google.com → Security → Third-party connections.
